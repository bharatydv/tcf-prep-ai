Already living in Canada on a work or study permit and need to find where to actually sit the TCF Canada exam? Here is the quick version: TCF Canada is administered almost exclusively through Alliance Française branches across the country, with a handful of university-based sites in smaller communities. Below is the confirmed national network, plus what to know before you book.

Important: not every Alliance Française branch runs TCF Canada on the same schedule, and some run TEF Canada instead or in addition. Session dates, fees and formats are set by each individual centre and change often. Always confirm directly on the specific centre’s own page before booking or travelling.

Toronto (GTA) has multiple TCF Canada locations

If you are in the Greater Toronto Area, Alliance Française Toronto runs TCF Canada sessions at four separate locations, not just the main Spadina Rd campus:

  • Downtown Toronto (main campus)
  • North York — 47 Sheppard Avenue East, Suite 502, North York, ON M2N 5N1
  • Mississauga
  • Oakville

Both paper-based and computer-based formats are offered depending on the session — you choose when you register. Sessions run quarterly, with registration opening about a month before each sitting, and you can only register for one session at a time.

Smaller cities and university-based centres

Beyond the major-city Alliance Française branches above, TCF Canada is also offered at a number of smaller, often university-affiliated sites — useful if you are outside a major metro and do not want to travel far. Confirmed examples include:

  • Lethbridge, AB — Dept. of Modern Languages, University of Lethbridge, 4401 University Drive, Lethbridge, AB T1K 3M4
  • St. John’s, NL — 232 Elizabeth Avenue, SN4039 Science Building, St. John’s, NL A1B 3X9
  • Iqaluit, NU — 4056 Anuri Street, P.O. Box 2178, Iqaluit, NU X0A 2H0

Sources tracking the national network put the total count at roughly 28+ accredited TCF Canada centres across every province and at least one territory, including smaller communities like Gaspé and Sept-Îles in Quebec. If your city is not listed above, it is worth checking the current full directory rather than assuming there is no option near you.

TCF Canada fees in Canada

Fees generally run $280–$320 CAD for all four mandatory modules — you cannot register section by section if you need an IRCC-recognized result. Some centres add a small administrative surcharge ($10–$25 CAD). Payment is typically by credit card or e-transfer at the time of booking, and fees are non-refundable in most cases.

How to book — step by step

  1. Pick the nearest centre from the list above, or the fuller provincial directory if you are not near a major city.
  2. Go to that centre’s own website directly — booking is handled individually by each Alliance Française branch, not centrally.
  3. Check for open sessions. Popular centres like Montréal and Toronto fill within 24–48 hours of a new session opening, so set a reminder or email alert if the centre offers one.
  4. Register online and pay to confirm your seat.
  5. Bring valid photo ID and your confirmation email on exam day.

Some centres run exam-prep workshops with priority booking — Alliance Française Vancouver, for example, offers a paid TCF workshop that includes a chance at priority access to an upcoming exam session, useful if you are worried about sessions selling out before you can book.

Choosing between TCF Canada and TEF Canada in Canada

Both are accepted equally by IRCC and map to the identical CLB/NCLC scale, so the real decision is about which format suits you and which centre is easier to reach — not which one Canada prefers. If your nearest Alliance Française branch runs TEF more frequently than TCF, that alone might be reason enough to choose it.

FAQs

How many TCF Canada exam centres are there in Canada?

Roughly 28+ accredited centres across every province and at least one territory, mostly run through Alliance Française, with a few university-affiliated sites in smaller cities.

Where can I take TCF Canada in Toronto?

Alliance Française Toronto runs sessions at four GTA locations: downtown Toronto, North York, Mississauga and Oakville. Both paper-based and computer-based formats are available depending on the session.

How much does TCF Canada cost in Canada?

Typically $280–$320 CAD for all four mandatory modules, plus a possible small administrative surcharge at some centres.

How far in advance should I book TCF Canada in Canada?

Book four to six weeks ahead if possible. Popular centres, especially Montréal and Toronto, can fill within 24–48 hours of a session opening.

Can I take TCF Canada outside a major city?

Yes — smaller, often university-affiliated centres exist in places like Lethbridge, St. John’s and Iqaluit, alongside Quebec communities like Gaspé and Sept-Îles. Check the full directory if you are not near a major metro.

Is TCF Canada computer-based or paper-based?

It depends on the centre and session — many centres, including Toronto, offer a choice between the two formats at registration.

Do all Alliance Française branches in Canada offer TCF Canada?

No — some branches focus more on TEF Canada, or run both on different schedules. Always check the specific test you need is being offered before booking a session.

Can I get priority access to a TCF Canada session if it is selling out?

Some centres, like Alliance Française Vancouver, offer paid prep workshops that include a chance at priority booking for an upcoming session — worth checking if your target dates are competitive.
